Step into the world as it was millions of years ago! Time Transit uses its cutting edge technology to send you back in time on an adventure of a lifetime. Navigate through mesmerizing landscapes from the depth of the oceans to the peaks of mountains. From small friendly dinosaurs to gentle giants, experience wildlife from bygone ages close up.

Whether you are looking for adventure, wildlife, tranquility, action or relaxation, you will find it waiting just a couple million years ago. Time Transit guarantees absolute safety during all its tours and places you in the safe hands of our automated tour guides!

Time Transit VR is a game for virtual reality developed by Freetime Studio. I was responsible for creating all of the creature animations, guide animations, tutorials, designing screen graphics and in the end, I even helped with the set-dressing.

Best months of my life.
